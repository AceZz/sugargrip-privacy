# SugarGrip Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 31 July 2026_

SugarGrip does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter — treat logs, craving check-ins, streaks, notes, and any
photos you attach — is stored **only on your device**. The app has no accounts,
no servers, and makes no network requests.

## What we receive

Nothing. We have no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no way
to see your data.

## Photos

If you attach a photo to a log, it is saved in the app's own storage on your
device. It is never uploaded anywhere.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. There is nothing
stored elsewhere to delete.

## Children

SugarGrip is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
